# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and consider different viewpoints.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus discussions on the project and the needs of its community.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work toward a constructive resolution.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, demeaning, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers at
`tianyan@chinatelecom.cn`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant,
depending on the severity and pattern of the behavior.
